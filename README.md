# ITA-Project-IPM06024
IIM Rohtak | IPM06 |  Section A | ITA Project | IPM06024 | J.S Shalakha Lal |




Problem Statement: 
Unemployment is a key indicator of economic health and labour market stability, yet its relationship with broader macroeconomic factors is often complex and difficult to interpret. While large datasets on economic indicators such as GDP growth, inflation, labour force participation, and population growth are widely available, understanding how these variables collectively influence unemployment across different economies remains a challenge.

This project analyzes unemployment trends using machine learning techniques and **World Bank** data for selected countries from 1991–2024. By examining the relationship between unemployment and key economic indicators (such as GDP growth, inflation, labour force participation rate, population growth, and unemployment rate) the study seeks to identify the most significant predictors of unemployment levels.

The project involves data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, and predictive modeling to examine how economic indicators influence unemployment levels. Several machine learning models, including Decision Trees, Random Forest, K-Nearest Neighbors, and Support Vector Machines, are implemented and compared.

Visualizations and feature importance analysis are used to identify the most influential factors affecting unemployment. Based on the results of the analysis, the project also provides recommendations on how economic indicators may be addressed to improve employment outcomes and labour market stability.
